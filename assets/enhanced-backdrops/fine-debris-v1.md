## Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: horizontally seamless game backdrop, photorealistic space debris. Generate a 3:1 wide panorama of very fine distant irregular rocky asteroid fragments, tiny gray-ochre glints against pure black space. A loose uneven belt extends horizontally across the exact vertical midpoint, spanning roughly the middle third of height, with irregular gaps and small clumps, never a solid dust cloud. Sparse tiny stars elsewhere. All fragments distant, none larger than 1 percent of image height. Top and bottom mostly black, no large foreground rocks, no planets, no moons, no nebula, no ground, no text, no glow haze. Same sparse density and illumination at left/right edges for horizontal wrapping. Realistic crisp rocky detail, subdued highlights, distinct from a dense large-rock asteroid belt.
