## Final prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: seamless horizontally wrapping game sky panorama, 3:1 landscape.
Primary request: Photorealistic replacement for a pale cyan daytime sky with small white cumulus clouds and a very low distant ridge of weathered gray rocky crags capped with vivid green moss and scrub.
Composition: almost all sky; wispy small cumulus mostly in lower half. The irregular rocky green ridge occupies ONLY bottom 12 percent, its base meeting bottom edge. No foreground ground, no brown terrain, no water. Level horizon, no perspective distortion. Distant low rock formations, not tall alpine mountains. Natural photographic geological texture.
Lighting: soft even daylight across the entire horizontal panorama; no vignette, no darker left half, no sun disc. Pale cyan clear top sky. Match both horizontal edges in color and ridge height for looping, without repeated identical rocks.
Constraints: no text, logos, buildings, people, spacecraft, snow, borders or watermark.
