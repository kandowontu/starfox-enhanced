## Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: photorealistic game background panorama, 3:1 wide. No text, logos, UI, people or ships. Sharp realistic physical detail, not painted or pixel art. Seamless horizontal repetition with matching left/right edge brightness and horizon height; no vignette, sun disc, lens flare, close foreground or side darkening. A vast inhabited-looking but unlit natural ocean planet seen from low orbit. The upper 70 percent of the image is empty black deep space with very sparse small white stars; no separate round planets or moons. At EXACTLY 70 percent image height a straight horizontal thin pale blue atmospheric limb, with no visible curvature across this narrow slice. The lower 30 percent is a continuous view of realistic mottled teal oceans, small muted green land areas and delicate swirling white cloud systems, extending all the way to the bottom edge. The planet is below the horizon, never a standalone globe. No repeated identical cloud patterns. This is a cylindrical strip that will wrap across an entire wide view; the horizon is perfectly level.
