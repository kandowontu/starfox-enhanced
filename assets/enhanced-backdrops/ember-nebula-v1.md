## Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: seamless horizontal game space backdrop, 3:1 wide panorama. Photorealistic telescope-like deep black space with sparse tiny amber and white stars and several separated small irregular red-orange emission nebula knots, finely branching wisps and glowing emberlike cores. Preserve abundant pure black negative space: nebulae occupy only about 20 percent of the image. Two or three different small nebula clusters across the width at varying heights, not a continuous cloud band. No blue or purple nebula, no planets, no large starburst, no spaceships, no text, no watermark, no horizon or ground. Tiny distant scale, sharp natural filament detail, not cartoon fire or smoke. Left and right edges both mostly black with matching sparse star density to tile horizontally; upper and lower edges black with sparse stars.
