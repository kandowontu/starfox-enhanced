## Prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: horizontally seamless distant desert mountain sky panorama for a game's optional realistic background replacement, 3:1 aspect ratio. Photorealistic pale lavender-blue daylight sky with thin high cirrus, atmospheric tan and rust-coloured desert mountains far away. The upper three quarters are sky. Irregular dry mountain skyline occupies only the bottom quarter, with layered rugged ridges, natural erosion and atmospheric haze, the base fading to warm dusty haze at the bottom edge. Do not include a foreground, ground plane, dunes near the viewer, water, vegetation, buildings, people or ships. No sun or moon disc, no lens flare, no vignette, no text, no logos or borders. Equal brightness and horizon altitude at both side edges, seamless horizontal tiling. Natural restrained colours and crisp realistic stone detail. This asset replaces sky and distant mountains only; gameplay terrain is rendered separately.
