Photorealistic thin irregular bank of distant bronze-orange storm clouds stretching horizontally across the exact middle of the image, occupying only central 12 percent of image height. Above and below the cloud bank is open deep crimson-red atmospheric haze, dark maroon close to the cloud band and brighter rich red toward both top and bottom edges. This is entirely sky, not a landscape or sunset over water. Preserve negative space, detailed natural billowing vapor along the narrow central band. Uniform exposure from left to right and naturally matching horizontal repeat edges, no vignette, no lens flare. No ground, mountains, water, planets, sun, stars, spacecraft, text, border or watermark.
