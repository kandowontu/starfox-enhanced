# Night coast panorama

Generated with the built-in image tool. PNG is the original output; BMP is
a 24-bit format conversion for runtime packaging. Water and source stars are
rendered independently. Intended for EX menu background 33.

## Final prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: seamless horizontal 3:1 game backdrop panorama, night coast, sky and distant terrain only.
Primary request: photorealistic low, irregular dark island ridges along a far-away coastal horizon beneath a clear deep midnight navy sky fading to a restrained pale peach-gray dusk glow near the horizon. Natural craggy silhouettes, subtle rocky texture, atmospheric haze, not tall mountains. Terrain occupies only the bottom 15 percent; top 85 percent is open sky. Bottom edge at the base of the islands, no visible foreground or sea surface (the game renders its own water). Equal illumination at both side edges, horizontally repeatable without a central spotlight or edge stripes.
Constraints: no moon, sun disk, buildings, city lights, spacecraft, people, text, borders or watermark. No stars in the bitmap; the game retains the source stars independently. Crisp realistic photography, not painted or low-poly. Do not make terrain oversized. Clear nearly uniform dark top sky, no vignette.
