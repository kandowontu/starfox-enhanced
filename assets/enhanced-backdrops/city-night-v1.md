## Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: photorealistic game background panorama, 3:1 wide. No text, logos, UI, people or ships. Sharp realistic physical detail, not painted or pixel art. Seamless horizontal repetition with matching left/right edge brightness and horizon height; no vignette, sun disc, lens flare, close foreground or side darkening. A vast distant futuristic industrial city skyline on a clear dark navy night sky. Upper three quarters: near-black navy sky with sparse tiny stars, no moon and no planets. Bottom quarter only: irregular clusters of distant metallic towers, antennae, angular factories and slim high rises, thousands of tiny warm gold/amber windows, restrained atmospheric haze at their feet. The buildings are very distant, with tallest towers reaching only 75 percent image height; bases reach the bottom edge and fade into charcoal haze. No foreground, streets, ground plane or water. Even panoramic scale, not one central iconic skyscraper. Physically realistic night photography look with controlled window highlights.
