# Ocean cloud panorama

Generated with the built-in image tool for Original Fortuna; no cartridge art
was supplied as an image input. PNG is the original; BMP is a 24-bit packaging
conversion. The separate source moon and water are not baked into this image.

## Final prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: seamless horizontal game sky panorama, 3:1 landscape bitmap.
Primary request: realistic towering white maritime cumulus cloud bank for an alien ocean-world background, replacing pixel-art clouds without adding ground.
Composition: sky only. Bottom edge is distant, low, misty cloud bases meeting a level invisible ocean horizon; no water visible. Varied billowing cloud towers rise through lower 55 percent, upper 45 percent mostly clear slate/periwinkle blue sky. Top sky approximately RGB 82,99,144, lower atmospheric haze cooler pale blue. Even soft daylight across the entire width, identical overall brightness at left and right edges, suitable for a horizontally repeating panorama. Fine photographic cloud texture and convincing volume, no giant foreground cloud.
Constraints: NO moon, sun, planet, stars, mountains, land, ocean surface, structures, spacecraft, text or watermark. The game renders its own single moon and water separately. No vignette, dramatic left/right lighting difference, lens flare, hard vertical seam or black borders.
