## Prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: horizontally repeating game sky/backdrop panorama, 3:1 aspect ratio. Create a photorealistic moonlit storm sky for a realistic replacement of an alien planet backdrop. Deep navy and muted violet layered thunderclouds with fine turbulent cloud detail, restrained silver illumination around their edges, distant atmosphere, subtle low mist. Upper 75 percent is clouded sky, lower quarter is increasingly hazy distant cloud bank, fading into a uniform cool slate haze at the bottom edge. No terrain or ground, no foreground, no buildings, no ships, no text, no logos, no borders. No visible moon or sun, no lightning. Uniform overall exposure from left to right, no darkened side, vignette or edge glare. Left and right edges should join seamlessly with the same colour and brightness. This is a distant background texture, not a full landscape photograph. Crisp realistic clouds, not painterly, not pixel art.
