## Final prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: horizontally seamless panoramic game sky/backdrop, 3:1 landscape. Create a photorealistic bright daylight coastal sky, clear soft periwinkle blue upper sky with a few thin high cirrus wisps, irregular white maritime cumulus banks along the low horizon, and a few very distant tiny low green islands along the bottom edge. Sky occupies upper 80%, cloud banks lower 20%, islands only bottom 4%. No water visible: image ends precisely at the island bases/horizon because the game renders water separately. Consistent exposure and color across entire width; left and right edges match naturally for horizontal repeat, no vignette or one dark side. Straight level horizon, no fisheye. No sun disc, moon, stars, buildings, ships, text, borders or watermark. Natural detailed clouds and atmospheric island haze, not pixel art, not stylized painting.
