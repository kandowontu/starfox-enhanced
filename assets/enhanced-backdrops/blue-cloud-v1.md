# Blue cloud for EX 1-4

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool on September 22, 2026. The native
EX preview 20 and 1-4 background contain an irregular blue gas cloud, not a
planet. The PNG master is `blue-cloud-v1.png`; `blue-cloud-v1.bmp` is the
same 1254×1254 RGB image packaged for the portable BMP decoder. No ROM art
is bundled in either file.

Prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: isolated enhanced-background sprite
> for a Star Fox-inspired space game's EX stage 1-4 and its background preview.
> Primary request: one irregular blue cosmic cloud formation, not a planet.
> Subject: a wispy, turbulent, asymmetrical cluster of deep cobalt and cyan
> gas, with a few tiny warm amber luminous flecks inside; loosely echoes an
> old pixel-art blue cloud sprite. Style/medium: high-detail cinematic
> space-cloud render with real volumetric gas texture, but clear readable
> silhouette at small display size. Composition/framing: cloud centered in a
> square frame, taking about 75% of the width; entirely surrounded by pure
> black space, feathered naturally into black; no hard circular edge or disk.
> Lighting/mood: luminous blue inner filaments against dark space. Constraints:
> single cloud only; do not draw a planet, sphere, horizon, moon, rock,
> asteroid, starfield, spaceship, text, logo, or watermark; no circular rim
> or planetary limb; black space outside the gas must be pure #000000.

The runtime uses the authored 48×48 atlas footprint at (80,264)-(128,312),
keeps the separate green limb native, and suppresses extra widescreen copies.
