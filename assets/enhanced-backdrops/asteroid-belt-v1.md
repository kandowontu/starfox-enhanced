# Central asteroid belt

Built-in image generation, September 20 2026. Resource 219, artwork 19.
Original output PNG; lossless 24-bit BMP runtime packaging. EX menu 6 only;
menu 17 is a different lower fine-debris field and is intentionally not mapped.

Prompt: Photorealistic seamless horizontal space asteroid-field panorama,
3:1. Pure black space, hundreds of small dark charcoal/brown irregular
cratered rocks with subdued cool blue edge light. Dense narrow distant belt
at exactly 50% height, uniform across width, with sparse slightly larger rocks
above/below and black top/bottom edges. Rocks smaller than 3% image height.
No planets, clouds, nebula, sun, ships, UI, text, bright stars or ground.
Not a ring or curved arc. Compatible repeating left/right edges.

Placement uses source row 400 minus the actual center BG2 scroll, preserving
the original middle band rather than repeating the belt at top and bottom.
