STAR FOX ENHANCED - PCVR DEVELOPMENT BUILD

Requires a Vulkan-capable GPU and an active OpenXR runtime. For Valve Index,
start SteamVR and select it as the active OpenXR runtime. Connect and wake
your headset/controllers before launching starfox_pcvr.exe.
An SDL-compatible Xbox, PlayStation or other standard gamepad can also be
used: left stick/D-pad steers; A fires, B bombs, X boosts, Y brakes; triggers
roll; Start pauses/opens the menu, Back changes view. Stick sensitivity is
adjustable in OPTIONS. Head tracking still requires the OpenXR headset.

Drag your own supported, unmodified retail Star Fox/Starwing ROM onto
BUILD-ASSETS.bat. The included builder creates Starfox-Assets.BIN beside
starfox_pcvr.exe. The builder and player are from the same build; do not reuse
a BIN from an older package. No ROM, generated BIN or music is included.
Do not distribute those files.

Launch with LAUNCH-PCVR.bat so missing-asset and runtime errors remain visible,
or use:
  starfox_pcvr --bundle "PATH/Starfox-Assets.BIN"
For optional MSU-1 music, put your Starfox-MSU1.PAK beside the executable or
beside the BIN passed with --bundle; it is detected automatically. You can
also pass --msu "PATH/Starfox-MSU1.PAK". Check pcvr-log.txt for the selected
pack and whether the saved startup music setting is ON.
The remaining optional argument is --data-dir "PATH".
Saves, preferences and shader cache are kept in vr-data beside the executable
unless --data-dir is provided. Extract to a writable folder. Existing flat-game
settings/saves are not modified. Ctrl+C in its terminal requests a clean exit.

This launches the game, not the 120-frame/30-second runtime diagnostic.
The VR startup menu selects Original/EX; headset session duration is unlimited.
Physical Index tracking, controls, comfort and sustained performance still
require validation. Successful compilation does not prove headset compatibility.
